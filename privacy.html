Smart Attendance Pro – Privacy Policy

Smart Attendance Pro helps a company record employee attendance and calculate salary. This page explains what the app stores and how it is used.

What we store

  • Admin: the Google account email and name used to sign in, the company name and logo.
  • Employees (added by the admin): name, employee ID, category, salary, overtime rate, bonus and Sunday settings.
  • Attendance: the date and time of each punch in and punch out.
  • Phone link: an anonymous technical ID that ties an employee's QR code to one phone, so nobody else can use it.
  • Feedback: the message you choose to send us, with your company name and email.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect location (GPS), contacts, photos or any other phone data. The camera is used only to scan QR codes.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Where data is kept

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and protected by security rules. An admin can see only their own company. An employee can see only their own record. Data travels over encrypted connections.

Who can see it

The admin sees the employees and attendance of their own company. Each employee sees only their own attendance and salary summary.

Keeping and deleting data

Company records are kept safely so that nothing is lost by mistake. Deleting an employee keeps their record in Delete History for the admin, who can restore it. To have your company data erased, send a request through Profile → Send Feedback inside the app.

Contact

For questions or requests, use Profile → Send Feedback inside the app.
